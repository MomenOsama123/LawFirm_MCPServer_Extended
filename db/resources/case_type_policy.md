# Case Type Policy

## Purpose
Defines the minimum requirements for each case type before it can proceed to review.

## Case Requirements

| Case Type | Minimum Seniority | Required Documents |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Civil | Associate | National ID, Claim Summary |
| Corporate | Senior | National ID, Company Registration |
| Family | Senior | National ID, Marriage Certificate |
| IP | Senior | National ID, Trademark Filing |
| Criminal | Partner | National ID, Police Report |

## Rules

- All required documents must be uploaded before the case moves to **Under Review**.
- Criminal cases require the highest seniority.
- Criminal cases with a detected conflict cannot proceed until reviewed by a Partner.
