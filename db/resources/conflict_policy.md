# Conflict of Interest Policy

## Purpose
Defines how conflicts of interest are identified and resolved during case intake.

## Rules
- A conflict exists if a client or opposing party matches a previous record by:
  - National ID or registration number.
  - Similar full name.
  - Related person or organization.

## Match Types
- **Exact Match:** Treated as a potential conflict.
- **Fuzzy Match:** Requires human review.
- **Related Party:** Requires human review.

## Resolution
- Only **Senior Associates** and **Partners** can resolve conflicts.
- Receptionists can view conflict status but cannot resolve it.
- Every resolution is recorded in the audit log.

## Outcomes
- **confirmed_conflict:** Case is rejected.
- **false_positive:** Case continues through the normal review process.
